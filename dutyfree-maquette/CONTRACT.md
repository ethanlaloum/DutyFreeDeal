# DutyFreeDeal maquette contract

Build a static, premium, airy marketplace in this directory. Existing older prototypes stay intact while this candidate is reviewed. No build or runtime library required. User has completed collaborative framing and authorized clearly labeled demo products, vendors and prices. FR and EN throughout. All transactions and verification actions are simulations; no backend and no messages/payments transmitted.

Confirmed: consumers buy; people and businesses sell after KYC/KYB adapted to seller origin. New physical goods initially. Global offers filtered by delivery destination; FR/ES/US/MX are demo markets, not an official launch list. Sellers manage stock and ship. Each vendor offer has its own detail page, even for identical items. Product price prominent, delivery separately shown. Single multi-vendor cart/payment. Large goods (cars/prefabricated houses) use quote/reservation flow rather than normal checkout. Both price advantage and finding unusual items equally important. Menu: all departments, deals, hard-to-find. Search prominent on arrival. Premium airy visual direction, preserve existing navy/gold identity. CORE deferred.

Open commercial rules: source of savings, price comparison reference, 15% net basis, membership terms, late-shipment penalties, verification country rules, customer service interlocutor. Do not invent operational rules for them. Surface unresolved settings in reviewer/admin views; no duty offsets, customs estimates or fake savings percentages. Root may ask exact quote/reservation modalities. Use request dialogs without deposits or confirmed bookings pending answer.
